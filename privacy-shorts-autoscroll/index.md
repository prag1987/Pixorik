# Privacy Policy: Shorts Auto Scroll

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Shorts Auto Scroll does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.

- The extension runs only on youtube.com and only checks the video playback position to move to the next Short.
- The only thing saved is your ON/OFF choice, stored locally in your browser using Chrome's storage. It never leaves your device.
- No analytics, ads, cookies or third-party services are used.

Contact: please open an issue on this repository.

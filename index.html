A process, not a person.

Every project runs through the same six stages, so quality never depends on who's at the desk that day.

  1. Plan

    We map what you're building, who it's for and the real problem, before a single screen is drawn.

  2. Design

    Wireframes to clickable prototypes, validated with you before anything gets built.

  3. Develop

    Clean, scalable engineering across mobile, backend and cloud, in short iterations.

  4. Test

    QA across devices and edge cases, so what ships works for real users.

  5. Deploy

    Store submission and release management on the App Store and Google Play.

  6. Review

    Monitoring, feedback and iteration. Then the cycle starts again for the next release.
